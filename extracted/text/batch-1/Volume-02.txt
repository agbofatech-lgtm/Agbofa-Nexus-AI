EXECUTIVE SUMMARY



This Volume 2 constitutes the complete Product Requirements Specification (PRS) for the
Agbofa Nexus AI platform. Where Volume 1 established the strategic foundation—the "why"—
this document defines precisely "what" we are building across every functional domain, user
interaction, and system behaviour.

The PRS has been authored by the Product and Technical leadership of the consulting
consortium with the explicit goal of eliminating ambiguity. Every feature, module, workflow,
user story, and functional requirement is defined at a level of detail sufficient for enterprise
architects, software engineers, AI engineers, QA teams, and future AI agents to implement
without requiring interpretation or guesswork.



Document Scope: This volume covers the complete functional and non-functional requirements
for all four programme phases (MVP Free Tier through Enterprise Platform). Requirements are
tagged by phase to enable incremental delivery without architectural compromise. Every
requirement has been validated against the strategic objectives defined in Volume 1.



Relationship to Other Volumes:



· Volume 1 (Strategy): Defines why we build. All PRS requirements trace to strategic objectives.

· Volume 2 (This Document): Defines what we build. Becomes the single source of truth for
requirements.

· Volume 3 (Architecture): Defines how the system is structured to meet these requirements.

· Volumes 4-8: Define implementation across AI, software, infrastructure, security, and
operations domains.



Critical Principle: No requirement exists in this document without a clear rationale explaining
why it is necessary and how it contributes to the strategic objectives. No feature is defined
without consideration of how it will scale from free-tier MVP through enterprise deployment.

2. PRODUCT VISION & STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT



2.1 Product Vision Statement



Agbofa Nexus AI is an autonomous AI-powered media company platform that discovers,
researches, verifies, produces, publishes, optimises, and monetises digital content across
multiple platforms with minimal human intervention. It is not a content tool—it is an AI media
operating system.



2.2 Product Mission



To provide a single, integrated platform that enables the operation of complete AI-native media
brands and empowers enterprise customers to deploy their own autonomous content
operations—delivering quality, scale, and efficiency impossible under human-only content
models.

3. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & ELEVATOR PITCH



3.1 Elevator Pitch



Agbofa Nexus AI is the operating system for autonomous media. It deploys collaborating AI
agents that handle the entire content lifecycle—from discovering what to write about, to
researching and verifying facts, to producing platform-optimised content, to publishing at the
perfect time, to optimising based on performance, to monetising every piece. One operator can
run what would traditionally require an entire media company. For enterprises, it's a white-label
platform that lets them launch their own AI-powered media operation under their brand, with
enterprise security, compliance, and scale. We're not building a better content tool—we're
building the company that builds the content.



3.2 Product Category



Category: Autonomous AI Media Operating System



Category Creation Rationale: No existing product category adequately describes Agbofa Nexus
AI. It is not:



· A content management system (CMS)

· A social media scheduler

· An AI writing assistant

· A content marketing platform

· A news automation tool

It is a new category: an operating system for autonomous media operations that subsumes and
integrates capabilities from all of the above categories into a single, coherent platform operated
by collaborating AI agents.



3.3 Core Product Differentiators



Differentiator Description Competitive Comparison

Full Autonomy End-to-end content lifecycle without human intervention per piece All
competitors require human operation per piece

Multi-Agent AI Architecture Specialised agents collaborating, not single-model generation Most
competitors use single-model prompt-and-response

Built-in Verification Truth-seeking design with automated fact-checking Competitors output
without verification

Platform-Native Optimisation Content specifically adapted for each platform's algorithm
Competitors offer basic format conversion

Content Provenance Complete auditable lineage for every piece No competitor offers machine-
readable provenance

Enterprise-Ready from MVP Architecture supports enterprise scale, security, multi-tenancy
Competitors retrofit enterprise features onto consumer tools

White-Label Capability Complete platform licensable to enterprises No autonomous media
competitor offers white-label

4. USER PERSONAS & USER STORIES

4.1 Persona Definitions



Full persona profiles were established in Volume 1, Section 14-15. This section provides the
product-specific persona details relevant to requirements definition.



Persona 1: Platform Administrator (PA)



· Role: Configures and manages the Agbofa Nexus AI platform instance

· Technical Level: High technical proficiency; understands AI concepts, APIs, cloud infrastructure

· Primary Goal: Ensure platform operates reliably, efficiently, and securely

· Key Tasks: Tenant configuration, agent monitoring, integration management, user
management, billing administration

· Phase Availability: P1-P4 (capabilities expand per phase)



Persona 2: Editorial Director (ED)



· Role: Sets editorial strategy and guidelines for one or more media brands

· Technical Level: Moderate; comfortable with dashboards and configuration interfaces

· Primary Goal: Ensure AI-produced content aligns with brand voice, editorial standards, and
strategic objectives

· Key Tasks: Editorial guideline definition, brand voice configuration, content strategy, exception
review, quality oversight

· Phase Availability: P1-P4



Persona 3: Content Reviewer (CR)

· Role: Handles exceptional cases requiring human judgement in the content pipeline

· Technical Level: Low to moderate; focused on content quality, not platform operations

· Primary Goal: Review and approve/reject/edit content flagged for human attention

· Key Tasks: Review queue management, content editing, quality judgements, feedback
provision

· Phase Availability: P1-P4 (volume of reviews decreases as autonomy increases)



Persona 4: AI Operations Manager (AOM)



· Role: Monitors and tunes AI agent performance

· Technical Level: High; understands AI/ML concepts, prompt engineering, agent behaviour

· Primary Goal: Optimise AI agent performance, diagnose issues, improve output quality

· Key Tasks: Agent performance monitoring, prompt tuning, model selection, behaviour analysis,
A/B testing

· Phase Availability: P2-P4



Persona 5: Business Analyst (BA)



· Role: Analyses content performance and business metrics

· Technical Level: Moderate; skilled in analytics tools and data interpretation

· Primary Goal: Provide data-driven insights for content strategy and monetisation

· Key Tasks: Performance analysis, audience insights, revenue reporting, competitive analysis

· Phase Availability: P2-P4



Persona 6: Enterprise Tenant Administrator (ETA)



· Role: Manages a white-label instance for an enterprise customer

· Technical Level: High; enterprise IT administration experience

· Primary Goal: Configure and manage enterprise deployment according to organisational
requirements

· Key Tasks: Tenant configuration, SSO integration, user management, security policy, SLA
monitoring

· Phase Availability: P3-P4



Persona 7: API Consumer / Developer (DEV)



· Role: Integrates Agbofa Nexus AI capabilities into external systems

· Technical Level: Very high; software development expertise

· Primary Goal: Successfully integrate platform APIs into applications and workflows

· Key Tasks: API integration, SDK usage, webhook configuration, error handling

· Phase Availability: P3-P4



Persona 8: End Content Consumer (ECC)



· Role: Consumes content published by Agbofa-powered media brands

· Technical Level: Variable; general digital literacy

· Primary Goal: Access high-quality, trustworthy content on topics of interest

· Key Tasks: Content consumption, engagement, feedback provision

· Phase Availability: P1-P4 (not a platform user, but a critical stakeholder)

5.1.1 Tenant Management



US-FND-001: [P1] Tenant Creation

As a Platform Administrator, I want to create and configure a platform tenant so that I can
establish an isolated environment for a media brand or enterprise customer.



Acceptance Criteria:



· AC1: Tenant creation requires name, identifier (slug), and initial admin user

· AC2: Tenant identifier must be globally unique across the platform

· AC3: Tenant creation provisions isolated data storage and configuration namespace

· AC4: Default configuration is applied from platform templates

· AC5: Tenant status can be: provisioning, active, suspended, archived



Functional Requirements:



ID Requirement Phase Priority

REQ-F-001 System MUST support multiple tenants with complete data isolation P1 Critical

REQ-F-002 Each tenant MUST have isolated configuration, content, analytics, and user spaces P1
Critical

REQ-F-003 System MUST support tenant creation via API and administrative interface P1 High

REQ-F-004 Tenant provisioning MUST complete within 5 minutes for standard configuration P1
Medium

REQ-F-005 System MUST support tenant suspension (read-only access) and reactivation P1 High

REQ-F-006 System MUST support tenant archival with data export capability P2 Medium

REQ-F-007 Enterprise tenants MUST support custom domain configuration [P3] P3 High

REQ-F-008 System MUST support tenant-level feature flags for phased capability rollout [P3] P3
High

REQ-F-009 Enterprise tenants MUST support dedicated infrastructure option [P4] P4 Medium

REQ-F-010 System MUST support tenant resource quotas and limits [P2] P2 High



R: Multi-tenancy is architecturally foundational. Even in Phase 1 with a single brand, the
platform must be designed for multiple tenants to avoid architectural redesign. Tenant isolation
is critical for enterprise customers.

· AC2: Users are assigned one or more roles within a tenant

· AC3: Role changes take effect immediately (within session refresh)

· AC4: User activity is logged for audit purposes

· AC5: Password policies and MFA can be configured



Functional Requirements:



ID Requirement Phase Priority

REQ-F-011 System MUST support user creation with email, name, and role assignment P1
Critical

REQ-F-012 System MUST support predefined role definitions (Admin, Editor, Reviewer, Viewer)
P1 Critical

REQ-F-013 System MUST support custom role creation with granular permission sets [P2] P2
High

REQ-F-014 System MUST support user invitation workflow with email verification P1 High

REQ-F-015 System MUST support Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) P1 Critical

REQ-F-016 System MUST log all user management actions for audit trail P1 High

REQ-F-017 System MUST support SAML 2.0 and OIDC for enterprise SSO [P3] P3 Critical

REQ-F-018 System MUST support SCIM for automated user provisioning [P3] P3 High

REQ-F-019 System MUST support session management (timeout, concurrent session limits) P1
Medium

REQ-F-020 System MUST support user activity audit log with search and export [P2] P2 High

5.2 CONTENT DISCOVERY & RESEARCH MODULE



Module Purpose: Enables autonomous discovery of content opportunities, trending topics, and

Module Owner: AI/ML Team

5.2.1 Trend Discovery & Monitoring



US-DIS-001: [P1] Automated Trend Detection



As an Editorial Director, I want the platform to automatically identify trending topics and
content opportunities so that our media brand can produce timely, relevant content without
manual trend monitoring.



Acceptance Criteria:



· AC1: System monitors specified sources for trending topics continuously

· AC2: Detected trends include topic, source, velocity (growth rate), and estimated relevance
score

· AC3: Trends are categorised by topic area based on brand configuration

· AC4: Trend detection runs at minimum every 15 minutes for high-priority sources

· AC5: Duplicate trends across sources are merged into single trend entities



Functional Requirements:



ID Requirement Phase Priority

REQ-F-031 System MUST continuously monitor configurable sources for trending topics P1
Critical

REQ-F-032 System MUST support source types: RSS feeds, social media APIs, news APIs, web
scraping P1 Critical

REQ-F-033 Each detected trend MUST include: topic, source URL(s), detection timestamp,
velocity score, estimated relevance P1 Critical

REQ-F-034 System MUST de-duplicate trends across multiple sources using semantic similarity
P1 High

REQ-F-035 Trend detection frequency MUST be configurable per source (minimum 15-minute
interval) P1 High

REQ-F-036 System MUST support keyword-based and topic-based trend filtering P1 High

REQ-F-037 System SHOULD predict trend trajectory (rising, peak, declining) using velocity
analysis [P2] P2 Medium

REQ-F-038 System MUST support trend source credibility scoring [P2] P2 High

REQ-F-039 System SHOULD identify cross-platform trend patterns and anomalies [P3] P3
Medium

REQ-F-040 System MUST log all trend detection activity for audit and improvement P1 Medium



R: Autonomous trend discovery is the entry point to the content pipeline. Without it, human
operators must manually identify content opportunities, defeating the autonomous value
proposition. Multi-source monitoring with deduplication is essential to avoid redundant content
generation.



---



US-DIS-002: [P1] Topic Opportunity Scoring



As an Editorial Director, I want discovered trends scored for content opportunity so that the
platform prioritises the most valuable topics for content production.

Acceptance Criteria:



· AC1: Each topic receives an opportunity score (0-100)

· AC2: Scoring considers: relevance to brand, audience interest prediction, competition level,
timeliness

· AC3: Scoring weights are configurable by Editorial Director

· AC4: Topics below configurable threshold are filtered out

· AC5: Score breakdown is visible for transparency



Functional Requirements:



ID Requirement Phase Priority

REQ-F-041 System MUST calculate opportunity score (0-100) for each identified topic P1 Critical

REQ-F-042 Opportunity scoring MUST consider: brand relevance, estimated audience interest,
content competition, timeliness/urgency P1 Critical

REQ-F-043 Scoring algorithm weights MUST be configurable per brand P1 High

REQ-F-044 System MUST support minimum opportunity score threshold for content pipeline
entry P1 High

REQ-F-045 System MUST provide score breakdown explaining contributing factors P1 Medium

REQ-F-046 System SHOULD learn from historical content performance to refine scoring [P2] P2
High

REQ-F-047 System MUST support manual topic addition with administrator-defined priority P1
High



R: Not all trends are worth pursuing. Automated opportunity scoring ensures resources are
allocated to highest-value content opportunities, directly supporting content quality and
audience growth objectives.

5.2.2 Research Execution



US-DIS-003: [P1] Automated Research Execution



As a Content Reviewer, I want the platform to automatically research selected topics so that
content production has accurate, comprehensive, and verified source material.



Acceptance Criteria:



· AC1: Research agent gathers information from multiple credible sources

· AC2: Research output includes: key facts, source attribution, confidence scores, contradictions
noted

· AC3: Research is structured with summary, detailed findings, and source list

· AC4: Research completion triggers content production pipeline

· AC5: Research sources are evaluated for credibility



Functional Requirements:



ID Requirement Phase Priority

REQ-F-048 System MUST execute multi-source research for each approved content topic P1
Critical

REQ-F-049 Research MUST include minimum configurable number of distinct sources (default:
3) P1 Critical

REQ-F-050 Each source MUST be evaluated for credibility score based on: domain authority,
factual accuracy history, citation practices P1 High

REQ-F-051 Research output MUST include: executive summary, key facts list, source
attributions, confidence scores per fact, identified contradictions P1 Critical

REQ-F-052 System MUST flag contradictions between sources for resolution P1 High

REQ-F-053 Research agent MUST respect robots.txt and source terms of service P1 Critical

REQ-F-054 System MUST support configurable research depth (quick, standard, comprehensive)
P2 High

REQ-F-055 System SHOULD cache research for related topics to reduce redundant source
fetching [P2] P2 Medium

REQ-F-056 System MUST log all source accesses with timestamps for provenance P1 High

REQ-F-057 Research MUST complete within configurable time limit (default: 10 minutes) P1
High

18. GLOSSARY OF PRODUCT TERMS



Term Definition

Agent An AI-powered software component that performs a specific function in the content
pipeline

Agent Orchestration The coordination and sequencing of multiple AI agents to complete
complex workflows

Brand Voice The configured personality, tone, and style characteristics that guide content
generation for a media brand

Canonical Content The primary, platform-agnostic version of a content piece before platform-
specific adaptation

Content Adaptation The process of transforming canonical content for specific platform
requirements

Content Brief Structured instructions generated from research that guide content production

Content Pipeline The end-to-end automated process from content discovery through
monetisation

Content Provenance Immutable record of the complete lineage and processing history of a
content piece

Confidence Score AI-generated assessment of the likely accuracy or quality of content (0-100)

Editorial Guidelines Human-defined rules governing content quality, style, sourcing, and ethical
boundaries

Hallucination AI-generated content that is factually incorrect or not supported by source
material

Human-in-the-Loop Pipeline configuration where content requires human approval at specified
stages

Human-on-the-Loop Pipeline configuration where content proceeds automatically with human
monitoring capability

Model Cascade Strategy of routing tasks to increasingly capable/expensive AI models based on
complexity

Opportunity Score Calculated value (0-100) representing the potential value of a content topic

Platform-Native Content specifically optimised for a particular platform's format, algorithm, and
audience

Quality Gate Automated checkpoint where content must meet defined thresholds to proceed

Research Brief Structured output of the research process containing facts, sources, and
confidence scores

Tenant An isolated instance of the platform for a specific brand or enterprise customer

White-Label Fully rebranded platform instance appearing as the enterprise customer's own
product

Workflow Defined sequence of agent operations and decision points in the content pipeline
